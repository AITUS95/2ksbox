## 1.0.0

The first release, on the Mac App Store and the Microsoft Store.

### Machines

- A launcher that makes Windows 98, Windows XP, Windows 7, Windows 11,
  DOS and other machines, each with hardware that suits it. Each
  machine opens in its own window.
- Windows 98 and DOS are emulated, on purpose, so old games see the
  kind of PC they were written for. On Windows and Linux, Windows XP
  and 7 can run on your computer's hypervisor.
- Windows 11 installs from Microsoft's own ISO with Secure Boot and a
  TPM 2.0, and shares the clipboard with your computer. On a Mac or a
  Linux computer it can share a folder too. On a Mac it is Windows 11
  on Arm.
- Snapshots of a machine turned off or running, kept as a tree, and
  clones.

### Games

- Direct3D from DirectX 1 to 9 on your graphics card, through the
  2ksbox adapter's driver on Windows 98 and XP.
- OpenGL games on your graphics card, through one file next to the
  game.
- An emulated 3dfx Voodoo 2 for Glide games, with 3dfx's own driver,
  from Windows 98 and from DOS.
- Disc images in cue/bin, CloneCD, Alcohol and ISO, or a folder, with
  copy-protected discs and CD audio. The disc shelf swaps discs while
  a machine runs.
- Sound Blaster 16 and General MIDI music, and the Roland MT-32 with
  ROMs you supply.
- USB gamepads, a gameport joystick, and a gamepad mapped to keyboard
  keys.

### The picture

- The guest's picture at its own resolution, scaled in whole steps,
  through a CRT shader you can tune and save as a profile per machine.

### Known limits

- The Mac App Store version needs macOS 26 and a Mac with Apple
  Silicon.
- Windows 7 is for its Aero desktop, not for games.
- Windows 11's display has no 3D acceleration. Its screen follows the
  window's size.
